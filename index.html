About

Dr. Jayapandian Natarajan is an academician, researcher, and innovator with a strong commitment to teaching, research, and student mentoring. His research interests encompass Cloud Computing, Cloud Data Security, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Internet of Things (IoT), and Network Security.

He has made significant scholarly contributions through SCI- and Scopus-indexed journal publications, international conference presentations, patents, and copyrights. His academic activities include research supervision, interdisciplinary collaborations, funded research projects, and mentoring students in research and innovation.

His research focuses on developing intelligent, secure, and scalable computing solutions to address emerging real-world challenges.
